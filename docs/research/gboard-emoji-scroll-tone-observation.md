# Emoji browse follow-up: bounded Gboard emulator observation

**Reference, not a source or license to copy Google code/assets.** On the shared 720×1600, 320 dpi Android 15 emulator, the installed Gboard in Android Settings search showed a single vertically scrolling emoji catalog. The ordinary keyboard and Viipio v0.9.0 were compared on the same screen. Screenshots under ignored `build/tooling/gboard-ref-*.png` and `build/tooling/viipio-v090-editor-before-emoji.png` are local observation evidence, not redistributable product art. The previous [Send-editor observation](gboard-emoji-observation.md) covers insertion/Backspace/Send; this note concerns scrolling, skin tone and icon proportions.

- The category rail stays at the top while the emoji grid scrolls **vertically across section headings**. Tapping a category jumps into the continuous list. At the People boundary, scrolling back into visible hand rows changed the highlighted tab from People to Smileys without another category tap. The visible section at the top, not merely the last tapped tab, drives selection in this setup.
- Tone-capable cells display **one** representative and a small corner marker, not six permanently adjacent variants. A long press on 👋 opened a floating horizontal strip of the neutral yellow emoji plus five skin tones. Selecting a medium-dark variant committed `👋🏾` to the synthetic Settings search editor and changed other supported hand cells' displayed tone too. Selecting the neutral option restored the reference tone; the two test emojis were removed from Settings search, leaving its prior synthetic `😀`. Gboard/Home and its previously selected IME were restored.
- In a separate no-network local `IME_ACTION_SEND` editor, holding a recent 👋 opened the six-tone popup without writing text. A single tap on a **different smiley outside that popup** dismissed it *and* inserted `😆` in the editor; it did not insert a tone choice. This is the observed Gboard outcome for that one tap, not a claim about every outside location or version. The probe returned to Home with Gboard still the default IME.
- The ordinary Gboard emoji symbol was visually about twice the diameter of the Viipio v0.9.0 outline face in the same approximate bottom-row cell. The reference icon is an observation of **proportion**, not an asset to reproduce byte-for-byte. Adjust only Viipio's original vector drawing scale inside its existing touch/gesture/accessibility bounds.
- Gboard's displayed catalog uses a less tiled, denser visual grouping than Viipio v0.9.0. Preserve original Viipio artwork, but compare column count, cell backgrounds, spacing and light/dark contrast on installed screenshots. The panel must remain inline and keep the host editor visible.

**Limits:** one Gboard build, one emulator, one theme and one editor. Exact typography, proprietary animations and behavior on every device are not established. A Viipio implementation must test scroll→active tab, tab→position, recents insertion/erasure, tone popup choice and cancellation, selection persistence, actual editor insertion, TalkBack actions, search isolation, and the unchanged bottom-row hit geometry. A physical Pixel 8 visual and touch comparison remains necessary.
